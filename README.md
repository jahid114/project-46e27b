# Eminent Sourcing Connect

Build a professional B2B corporate website for a Bangladesh-based garments buying house called "EMINENT SOURCING LTD" — a 100% export-oriented RMG (readymade garments) sourcing company. The audience is international apparel buyers/importers in the USA, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand and Europe, so the tone must read as credible, established, and trustworthy — not like a small local vendor site.

BRAND

- Company: Eminent Sourcing Ltd

- Slogan: "YOUR TRUST IS OUR STRENGTH"

- Address: Ali Plaza-2, 5th Floor, 16 Lalchand Road, Chawak Bazar, Chittagong, Bangladesh

- Colors: deep navy #16233F (primary), warm gold #C6A15B (accent), ivory/off-white #F7F4ED (background), charcoal #2B2B2B (body text)

- Typography: strong, condensed bold sans-serif for headings (Arial/Helvetica-style, wide letter-spacing on the wordmark), clean regular-weight sans for body text. No decorative or script fonts — this is a corporate export/manufacturing brand.

- Logo: an "ES" monogram badge (navy circle, gold ring, ivory letterforms, dashed stitch-line accent) paired with the wordmark "EMINENT SOURCING / LTD". I'll upload the logo files — use them in the header and footer.

- Visual direction: flat, confident, editorial-corporate — think large fabric/garment photography, generous whitespace, thin gold hairline dividers, no gradients, no rounded "SaaS card" clutter, no stock-photo cheesiness.

SITE STRUCTURE (single multi-section homepage + a few subpages)

1. HEADER / NAV

Logo left, nav links: Home, About Us, Services, Products, Quality & Compliance, Contact. A prominent "Request a Quote" button in gold, top right.

2. HERO

Full-width hero with a large garment/textile production image or fabric texture as background (dark navy overlay for text contrast). Headline built around the slogan and positioning: 100% export-oriented garments buying house from Chittagong, Bangladesh, connecting global apparel buyers to reliable, quality-controlled RMG production. Two CTAs: "Request a Quote" (primary, gold) and "View Our Products" (secondary, outline).

3. ABOUT / INTRODUCTION

Company overview: established 2026, 100% export-oriented garments buying house in Chittagong, strong supply channel, capacity to handle large-quantity orders at competitive prices within short lead time. Mention target markets: USA, Canada, UK, Australia, New Zealand, Europe. Skilled team of merchandisers and QA inspectors overseeing delivery time, production supervision, and quality control.

4. VISION & GOAL

Two-column or two-card layout.

Vision: "Through our services we create future values for sustainable growth. We believe clients' satisfaction is our key performance parameter. We work for long-term business relationships through highest dedication and professionalism."

Goal: "Excellent integration of marketing, merchandising expertise, and ethical standards to establish a confident platform for our valued customers."

5. CORE VALUES

Seven-item grid/row (icon + short label + one-line description), each on a word from the company's actual values — do not invent generic ones:

- Excellence — committed to maintaining work excellence

- Meaningful — our effort must have a sound meaning

- Integrity — integrity is our motto

- Novelty — eagerness for innovation

- Efficient — we move together with our efficiency

- Nourish — we nourish your feedback and suggestions

- Trust — we move forward with your trust

6. WHAT WE DO / SERVICES

Present as a structured process (this is a sourcing/merchandising company, so show the full value chain):

- Fabric sourcing & yarn/fabric procurement monitoring

- Factory selection & evaluation (capacity/capability + labor law and environmental compliance)

- Sampling (own sample house, buyer design/sketch based)

- Price scrutiny & realistic cost negotiation

- Quality control during production (fabric approval, in-line inspection, surprise factory checks, pre-shipment inspection & certificate of inspection)

- Full coordination to shipment (lab-dip approval, LC/banking, customs, GSP documentation, shipping, bank-to-bank documentation)

- Regular production/quality reporting to buyers

- Buyer visit assistance (hotel booking, supplier meeting arrangement)

Present this as a clean numbered process/timeline component, not a wall of text.

7. PRODUCT RANGE

Tabbed or three-column layout by category:

- Circular Knit (Jersey Wear): basic/fancy T-shirts, polo, raglan, cut & sew, nightwear, tank tops, turtleneck/mock neck, shorts, underwear, sweatshirts — for men, ladies, boys, girls, babies

- Woven: shirts, pants, denim pants, cargo/Bermuda shorts, chino, jackets, trousers, aprons, workwear, outdoor wear

- Knitwear/Sweater: cardigans, pullovers, shrugs, jumpers (half/full zip, round/V/high neck)

Mention fabric range briefly (single jersey, rib, interlock, pique, fleece, yarn-dyed stripes for knits; cotton twill, canvas, flannel, denim varieties for woven) — summarize, don't dump the full spec table on the page; offer a "Download Product Catalogue" or "Request Fabric Specifications" CTA instead.

8. QUALITY & COMPLIANCE

Highlight certifications/standards familiarity: ISO, WRAP, BSCI, SEDEX, Oeko-Tex, Accord, Alliance. Mention third-party inspection acceptance (Bureau Veritas, TÜV, ITS, SGS). Also cover labor/ethical standards: no child labor or forced labor, safe and hygienic working environment. Present as a trust/compliance badge grid.

9. SHIPMENT & PAYMENT TERMS (brief, buyer-facing summary)

- Delivery: based on L/C reception date, ~60 days + transit for knitted garments (45 days possible depending on quality/situation)

- Shipment: FOB or C&F, FCL/LCL, by sea or air; sea transit to Europe ~3–4 weeks, air ~1 week

- Payment: L/C at sight, irrevocable and transferable, UCPDC (ICC Publication) compliant

Present as a short icon-based summary, not the full banking clause text — keep it buyer-friendly, not a legal document.

10. CONTACT / INQUIRY

Contact form (Name, Company, Country, Email, Phone, Product Interest, Message/Order details) with a clear "Send Inquiry" CTA. Show company address (Ali Plaza-2, 5th Floor, 16 Lalchand Road, Chawak Bazar, Chittagong, Bangladesh), email, phone, and an embedded map. Add note that inquiries are usually responded to within 24–48 hours.

11. FOOTER

Logo + slogan, quick nav links, contact details, social links (LinkedIn placeholder), copyright line.

FUNCTIONAL / UX REQUIREMENTS

- Fully responsive (mobile-first, since buyers may browse on mobile while traveling)

- Fast-loading, optimized images with lazy loading

- Sticky header with visible "Request a Quote" CTA at all times

- SEO-friendly structure: proper heading hierarchy, meta title/description slots per page, alt text on all images — this site needs to rank for terms like "garments buying house Bangladesh," "RMG sourcing agent Chittagong," "knitwear supplier Bangladesh export"

- Accessible: sufficient color contrast (navy/gold/ivory should meet WCAG AA), keyboard-navigable nav and forms

- No stock-photo clichés or generic AI-page tells (no cookie-cutter rounded cards everywhere, no matching drop-shadows on every element, no filler "Lorem ipsum" — use the real content above)

- Include a WhatsApp/direct inquiry floating button, common for South Asian B2B export sites reaching international buyers

Build this as a clean, modern, editorial-style corporate site — confident and understated, letting the navy/gold palette and real content carry the credibility rather than heavy decoration.
